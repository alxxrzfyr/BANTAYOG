Zero Diversion — Zero Paperwork

With traditional unrestricted cash, there is no way to know where funds ultimately go — they may pay off store debt or purchase items that provide little nutritional value. Bantayog enforces food eligibility item-by-item at the counter and settles every transaction on Polygon Amoy in real time.

AI-Powered Product Recognition

Google Gemini Vision identifies product brand and name — including both branded and non-branded items — from a photo. The Approved Food List then decides eligibility, locking funds strictly to nutrient-dense, government-approved items.

Polygon Blockchain Settlement

Every transaction is recorded as an immutable, itemized receipt on Polygon Amoy — creating full item-by-item transparency for government nutrition subsidies.

Zero Paperwork for Merchants

Sari-sari stores receive instant digital settlement and reconciled receipts. No voucher bundles, no manual audits, and no expensive POS hardware required.

Zero Smartphone for Guardians

Guardians only need their QR Nutri-Pass — no phone or data plan required. The merchant handles all scanning on a low-cost Android phone at the counter.

Automated Tiering Engine

Age-Based Subsidy Tiering

The First 1,000 Days spans from conception through 730 days post-birth — the window with the greatest impact on a child's lifelong brain and physical development. The primary difference between the two tiers is the monthly credit amount. All funds remain 100% purpose-bound to approved nutritious food in both tiers.
